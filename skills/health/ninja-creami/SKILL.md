# Ninja Creami

## When to Use
- Any Ninja Creami question: recipes, settings (Milkshake vs Ice Cream), container/outer-bowl mechanics, stabilizer dosing, texture fixes (crumbly/gummy/thin).
- Building high-protein frozen treats or protein-shake drinks from milk bases, and comparing protein-milk vs whey-powder economics.

High-protein frozen treats (ice cream / milkshakes) from the Ninja Creami, especially milk-based and protein-milk bases.

## Hardware (standard NC301 kit)
- **Pints** (16 oz / 473 ml) = the containers you FILL and FREEZE. Fill only to the **MAX FILL line** (~⅔ up) — overfilling strains the motor and gives a gummy mess. 2 L of base ≈ 2 pints per freeze; keep the rest in the fridge, spin, re-pour, refreeze.
- **Outer bowl** (the "weird mug thing" with a handle) = processing cradle. The frozen pint drops inside it; it is NOT a storage container and never gets liquid poured into it. The pint looks barely half full inside it — normal.
- **Blade** (Creamerizer paddle) mounts in the outer bowl's LID: press latch, insert paddle, release latch (it should feel loose). Lock lid onto bowl (align arrows, twist clockwise), place on motor base handle-centered, twist handle RIGHT to raise platform + lock.
- First-time users routinely confuse the outer bowl with a container — explain the pieces before recipes.

## Core workflow
1. **Stabilizer in first** (see dosing) — blend into cold liquid with a stick blender or shake hard; let hydrate ~5–10 min, shake again before pouring. Xanthan clumps instantly in cold milk if dumped in.
2. Pour to MAX FILL line, lid on, freeze **flat** 24 h (18 h min — under-frozen = gummy; angled freeze = hard block).
3. Rest the pint **10 min** on the counter before spinning.
4. Spin: Ice Cream / Lite Ice Cream for scoops; **Milkshake** for thick drinkable shakes (designed for liquid-y bases; no stabilizer strictly needed but it thickens and slows melting).
5. Fixes: crumbly/dry → poke hole to bottom, add 1–2 tbsp liquid, **Re-spin** (repeat once). Thin → add stabilizer next batch. Low-fat bases melt fast — drink immediately.

## Stabilizer dosing (per pint ≈ 450 ml)
- **Xanthan gum**: ¼ tsp (ice cream) / ⅛ tsp (milkshake) — ≈ 1 tsp per 2 L carton of base. Overdose = gummy/snotty texture (the main failure mode).
- Cream cheese: 1 tbsp (softened) — best texture, adds ~50 cal.
- Sugar-free pudding mix: 2 tbsp (stabilizer + flavor).

## Milk-based base notes
- Plain milk alone freezes icy/crumbly (low fat + low solids) → stabilizer basically required.
- High-protein ultrafiltered milk (e.g. Natrel Plus, 18 g/250 ml) creams noticeably better than regular milk — milk protein acts as a natural stabilizer — but still needs a stabilizer when sugar is low (13 g/cup is lean for ice cream).
- Typical macros (protein chocolate milk, ~450 ml pint): ~270 cal, ~32 g protein, ~25 g sugar.

## Pitfalls
- Xanthan in cold liquid = instant clumps → always blend/shake + hydrate first.
- Overfill past MAX FILL line = motor strain / gumminess.
- Freeze <18 h = gummy/slushy.
- Liquid goes in the PINT only, never the outer bowl.
- "Mug + two smaller containers" confusion = outer bowl vs pints (see Hardware).
