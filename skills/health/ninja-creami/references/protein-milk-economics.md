# Protein milk economics & availability (verified Aug 2026, Halifax NS)

## Natrel Plus — the "enriched" lactose-free chocolate milk
- Product: **Natrel Plus Chocolate 1%** — lactose-free, ultrafiltered partly skimmed milk + water, sugar, cocoa. No added protein powder, no sucralose; protein boost comes from ultrafiltration (less dilution), not fortification.
- Per 250 ml (1 cup): **18 g protein**, 150 cal, 2.5 g fat, 13 g sugar, 550 mg calcium. 2 L carton = 8 servings = 144 g protein total.
- vs regular **Natrel LF Chocolate 1%**: 9 g protein, 120 cal, 14 g sugar, 300 mg calcium.
- vs **Natrel LF plain 1%**: 9 g protein, 90 cal, 7 g sugar, 300 mg calcium.
- Natrel Plus also comes in Vanilla 1%, plain 2%, plain 3.25% — all 18 g/250 ml, plain versions without added sugar (2% = 160 cal, 9 g sugar).

## Decision rules
- vs regular chocolate milk: enhanced is 2× protein for +30 cal and LESS sugar (13 vs 14 g) → clearly better for protein.
- Protein quality: complete milk protein, ~80/20 casein:whey. Slow-absorbing vs whey — only matters for post-workout timing, not total daily protein.
- vs whey-powder route: wash on cost for smoothies; enriched milk WINS for ice cream/shakes (liquid + protein in one, no powder clumps, smoother freeze).
- Sugar caveat: 13 g/cup adds up if consumed daily; powder + plain milk gives sugar control.
